# PARADOX PROTOCOL — ASTERIA

## Status

All currently supported Paradox Pokémon are present in the fauna database, but none are ordinary wildlife.

They use encounter class:
- `paradox_past`
- `paradox_future`

## Rule

A Paradox encounter requires a **temporal anomaly world chain**.

The world must first establish a cause: spatial-temporal instability, research incident, artifact, energy event or other campaign-grounded mechanism.

The player does not get access merely by reaching a level.

## No random spawning

Paradox Pokémon:
- do not appear in routine route rolls;
- do not breed into normal Asteria ecology;
- are not stocked by normal traders;
- do not become common after first discovery.

A temporary breach can close.

## Difficulty

Do not level-scale a Paradox Pokémon down merely because a low-level player reaches the anomaly.

Use danger signals and the real Pokémon 5e stat block.

Retreat is allowed.

## Capture

If capture is mechanically/legal-fictionally possible, use normal Pokémon 5e capture rules.

The script never guarantees capture.

## Hidden placement

Exact environmental anchors are kept in:

`gm_private/ASTERIA_SPECIAL_FAUNA.json`

Do not reveal them until the campaign legitimately discovers the anomaly.
