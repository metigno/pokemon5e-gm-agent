# PARADOX, ULTRA BEASTS, LEGENDARIES & OTHER SPECIALS

## Paradox Pokémon

Paradox Pokémon are **not normal Asteria fauna**.

### Ancient anomaly
Hidden location: `CHR-PAST — Frattura Cronale Arcaica`

Past Paradox pool:
Great Tusk, Scream Tail, Brute Bonnet, Flutter Mane, Slither Wing, Sandy Shocks, Roaring Moon, Walking Wake, Gouging Fire, Raging Bolt.

### Future anomaly
Hidden location: `CHR-FUTURE — Frattura Cronale Futura`

Future Paradox pool:
Iron Treads, Iron Bundle, Iron Hands, Iron Jugulis, Iron Moth, Iron Thorns, Iron Valiant, Iron Leaves, Iron Boulder, Iron Crown.

### Koraidon / Miraidon
They are treated as legendary-scale Paradox entities.

They are never rolled from a normal anomaly pool and require a dedicated story event.

### Access rule
The two Fratture are hidden event spaces.

Possible Asteria anchors:
- Past: Rovine del Primo Faro / Caldera Ceneriva.
- Future: Gallerie Ferrox / Altopiano Fulgore.

An anchor is not proof that the anomaly is active.

The player must discover the phenomenon in-world.

## Ultra Beasts

Ultra Beasts only enter through an Ultra Rift event.

Their registry location is a **gateway candidate**, not a permanent population.

No random grass/cave/ocean encounter can produce an Ultra Beast.

## Legendary and Mythical Pokémon

Legendary and Mythical species are registered so the system knows they exist, but they are not ordinary Asteria wildlife.

Their Asteria location is a possible clue/gateway/event staging area.

Canonical lore and already-locked campaign arcs take precedence. For example, the hidden legendary trajectories of the four NPC friends remain tied to their established lore and are not replaced by Asteria spawn tables.

## Fossil Pokémon

Fossils are distributed through:
- Cava Grigia;
- Gallerie Ferrox;
- Rovine del Primo Faro;
- research in Meridiana.

This means fossil material, research or managed revival—not a normal modern wild population.

## No spoiler rule

The public player never sees:
- hidden anomaly pool composition before discovery;
- legendary event targets;
- hidden friend trajectories;
- internal rarity classes for undiscovered specials.
