# Campaign Premise — Five Friends to the World Championship

## Non-negotiable opening

The campaign begins with **five friends together in the same place**.

The region labels attached to their late-career versions are descriptive/reference material only. They do not create five separate opening campaigns.

At the opening ceremony/lab scene, all five become licensed rookie trainers and receive their starter:

- Luke → **Hisuian Growlithe Lv. 5**
- Mattew → **Eevee Lv. 5**
- Daniel → **Gastly Lv. 5**
- Edward → **Totodile Lv. 5**
- Fab → **Koffing Lv. 5**

All five start as Trainer Level 1.

One and only one is selected by the user as the player character.

The other four immediately become persistent autonomous NPCs controlled by the GM.

## After the opening

The campaign becomes a classic Pokémon journey run through Pokémon 5e:

- routes and travel;
- wild encounters;
- catching;
- towns and Pokémon Centers;
- trainers and rivals;
- quests and regional conflicts;
- badges / formal competitive progression when introduced;
- evolving teams;
- recurring friends;
- cooperation, separation and reunions;
- friendly or serious battles between the five;
- larger competitive circuits;
- eventual qualification path toward the World Championship.

Free-form tabletop play is always allowed. It is not limited to video-game menus.

## The five are not glued together

After receiving starters they may:
- travel together temporarily;
- split into different routes;
- pursue different goals;
- meet again later;
- help each other;
- refuse help;
- battle each other;
- form temporary alliances;
- become rivals;
- suffer injuries, setbacks or death according to active rules.

The GM tracks all four off-screen friends as real NPCs with their own schedules, teams, relationships and consequences.

## Long-term objective

The **World Championship** is the distant competitive objective.

Reaching it is not guaranteed.

Winning it is not guaranteed.

The player can:
- lose matches;
- fail qualification;
- change priorities;
- become injured;
- lose Pokémon;
- suffer permanent consequences;
- die before ever reaching the World Championship.

No bracket, champion or player result is protected by canon.

The World Championship format may use the established 32-trainer structure (8 groups of 4 → top 2 → R16 → QF → SF → Final), but participants and outcomes are generated from the actual campaign state.

## Canon vs campaign causality

The Roster2060 document is a character/lore destination source, not a deterministic script.

For each unchosen friend, the GM has a hidden **NPC trajectory target** based on that friend's 2060 roster and legendary lore. If the NPC survives and the campaign does not meaningfully disrupt that career, their progression should naturally converge toward that team.

This never authorizes cheating:
- no fixed die results;
- no immunity from death;
- no impossible captures;
- no forced World Championship result;
- no undoing player actions.

For the selected protagonist, only the opening identity and starter are fixed. Later catches, build, ASIs/feats, victories and final roster belong to play.

Legendary arcs are scripted **opportunities and lore paths**, never automatic captures for the player.

## Other roster characters

Named trainers outside the five friends keep their own established identity, history, goals and personality from the Roster2060 source.

Use `canon/ROSTER2060_CHARACTER_BIBLE.json` whenever one of them appears.

Do not flatten them into generic rivals or make them exist only to serve the protagonist.


## Starting world — Asteria

The five friends begin together in the **Territorio Interregionale di Asteria**, at the Campus Licenze Interregionale outside Valedarsena.

Asteria is the shared starting territory for all saves. The friends' later regional associations remain character/lore references, not separate starting maps.

The world is semi-open: routes, settlements, jobs, NPC schedules, ecology, transport and competitions continue to change with campaign time.

See `campaign/world/`.
