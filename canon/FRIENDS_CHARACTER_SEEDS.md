# Five Friends — Character Seeds

This file adapts the late-career 2060 document backward into rookie personalities.

The 2060 material defines who these people can grow into. It does **not** mean they already possess their future achievements, teams or maturity at the campaign opening.

## Luke

**Starter:** Hisuian Growlithe, Level 5.

Core seed: instinctive and competitive; forms a deep bond with his starter and prefers concrete pressure over sterile control. Even early on, Luke is good at reacting when a situation changes, but he can accelerate one turn too early and spend a resource that would have mattered later.

Rookie expression: energetic, adaptable, emotionally invested in his Pokémon, willing to improvise.

## Mattew

**Starter:** Eevee, Level 5.

Core seed: methodical, disciplined, studies patterns and prefers calculated risks. He naturally thinks about timing, positioning and sequence.

Rookie weakness: genuine chaos or opponents who behave without a readable pattern can slow his decision-making.

## Daniel

**Starter:** Gastly, Level 5.

Core seed: plans in layers, loves reading intentions and constructing traps. He sees branches other people miss.

Rookie weakness: over-analysis. Against an opponent doing something irrational or deliberately simple, he may search for a deeper reason that is not there.

## Edward

**Starter:** Totodile, Level 5.

Core seed: direct, aggressive, resilient and experiential. He learns fastest by actually fighting.

Rookie weakness: long-horizon planning. He can accept a favorable-looking exchange without fully valuing the resource he is giving up for later.

## Fab

**Starter:** Koffing, Level 5.

Core seed: tenacious, affectionate toward his Pokémon and patient. He naturally accepts gradual progress and is comfortable winning through control, chip and persistence.

Rookie weakness: he can wait too long before changing from preparation/control into decisive action.

## Source usage

These seeds are derived from the 2060 character sections. The GM should preserve their identity while allowing childhood/rookie immaturity, relationships, victories, trauma, failure and player interaction to change how that identity develops.
